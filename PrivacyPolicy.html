💎

ShowScout Pro & Subscription Data

When you subscribe to ShowScout Pro, the following data practices apply:

  • Payment Processing: All payments are processed securely by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We never see, store, or have access to your credit card or payment details.
  • Subscription Management: We use RevenueCat, a trusted third-party service, to manage subscription status and entitlements.
  • Anonymous Identifiers: RevenueCat uses anonymous app user IDs to track your subscription status without collecting personal information.
  • Receipt Validation: Purchase receipts are validated with Apple/Google servers to verify your Pro status.
  • Cross-Device Sync: Your subscription status syncs across devices signed into the same Apple ID or Google account.

Your subscription data is only used to: Verify your Pro status, restore purchases across devices, and provide customer support if you contact us about subscription issues.

What RevenueCat Collects:

  • Anonymous app user ID (randomly generated)
  • Subscription status and transaction history
  • Device type and OS version (for compatibility)
  • Country (for currency and tax purposes)

RevenueCat does NOT have access to your name, email, or payment card details. Learn more at revenuecat.com/privacy

AI-Powered Features

ShowScout uses AI to provide personalized movie recommendations. Here's how it works:

  • When you use the AI recommendation feature, your mood or preference prompt is sent to Groq's AI service
  • Prompts are processed in real-time and are NOT stored by ShowScout
  • No personal information is included in AI requests
  • Groq may process data according to their privacy policy

Learn more about Groq's privacy practices at groq.com/privacy-policy

Data Storage & Retention

Here's how long we keep different types of data:

  • Local Preferences: Stored on your device until you clear app data or uninstall
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated anonymously, retained for up to 26 months
  • AI Prompts: Not stored - processed and discarded immediately
  • Subscription Data: Retained by RevenueCat as long as your subscription is active, or as required for transaction records

Your Rights

You have control over your data. Here are your rights:

  • Access: Request information about what data we process
  • Deletion: Clear local data by clearing app cache or uninstalling the app

  • Subscription Management: Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple/Google account settings

To request subscription data deletion: Contact us at the email below with your request. Note that some transaction data may be retained by Apple/Google and RevenueCat for legal and accounting purposes.
